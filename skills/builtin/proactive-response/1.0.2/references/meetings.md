# Meetings, interviews and appointments

One meeting is one Tracking Item of kind `meeting`, with `due_at` its start time. An interview, a podcast recording or a call with an investor or client is a meeting too.

## Routines it usually needs

Decide from what the meeting needs, not from a fixed set:

- Only remembering it matters: one reminder about 30 minutes before (longer when the Human has to travel).
- Worth preparing (an interview, a first meeting, an investor, client or partner, or one where the Human presents or decides): a Routine early enough for the Human to read what you prepare, such as the evening before or a few hours ahead. A short reminder close to the start can follow when it helps.
- An appointment that must be confirmed or booked in advance: one reminder when that should happen.
- The evening before, when the next day holds several meetings or people the Human meets for the first time: one look at the whole day.

## When things change

- Rescheduled: update the item's `due_at` and move its Routines.
- Cancelled, or its time has passed: mark the item done and delete remaining Routines. A reminder before the meeting does not finish it.
